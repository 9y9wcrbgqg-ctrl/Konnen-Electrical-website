Capabilities

Electrical work,
thought through.

Konnen is structured around the work itself — what needs to be understood, planned, installed, repaired, or improved — rather than a bloated list of service categories.

01 · Project work

Build it with a plan.

01A

New Installations

Electrical work for new spaces, additions, site improvements, equipment, and infrastructure where coordination matters from the beginning.

01B

Remodels & Upgrades

Existing systems assessed carefully before new work is layered in, with attention to what is staying, what is changing, and what needs correction.

01C

Equipment & Dedicated Power

Power requirements translated into practical installation plans for equipment, specialty loads, and future capacity.

02 · Service & troubleshooting

Find the cause, not just the symptom.

02A

Troubleshooting

Systematic diagnosis when circuits, equipment, controls, or electrical systems are behaving unpredictably or failing altogether.

02B

Repair & Replacement

Targeted corrective work based on the actual condition of the system, with clear communication around what is necessary and why.

02C

Electrical Improvements

Practical updates for reliability, usability, maintainability, and the changing electrical demands of a property or operation.

03 · Power & systems

See the whole electrical path.

03A

Distribution

Electrical distribution approached with attention to source, load, pathway, protection, accessibility, and future serviceability.

03B

Controls & Connections

Equipment connections and control work coordinated so individual components operate as part of a complete system.

03C

System Coordination

Electrical decisions made with the broader job in mind — sequencing, access, other trades, equipment requirements, and the finished result.

“The best electrical work starts before the first conductor is pulled.”

Konnen’s approach is simple: understand the system, build the plan, then execute the work with precision.
