Common questions

Before you call.

Do you take small service and troubleshooting calls?

Yes. Troubleshooting and repair work is part of the company’s core capability, subject to current scheduling and location.

Do you handle both homes and businesses?

Konnen evaluates residential, commercial and project electrical work. The scope is reviewed first so the right fit is clear before anything is scheduled.

Can you work from plans or coordinate with a builder?

Yes. The electrical background behind Konnen includes plan reading, material planning, coordination with other trades and project installation work.

What should I include when requesting an estimate?

Share the project location, what you want changed or repaired, any known equipment information, your timing and photos when they help explain the condition.
