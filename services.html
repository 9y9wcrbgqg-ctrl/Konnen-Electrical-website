Electrical services

The work is electrical.
The approach is deliberate.

Konnen is structured around the electrical work Southern Oregon homes, businesses, builders and property owners actually need—not a narrow specialty list.

01

Troubleshooting & repairs

Find the cause before replacing parts.

Electrical problems often show up somewhere other than where they start. A tripping breaker, dead circuit, flickering light or unreliable piece of equipment may be caused by a connection, load, conductor, device or upstream condition.

Konnen approaches troubleshooting as a system problem: gather symptoms, test what matters, isolate the fault and explain the repair path before the work grows unnecessarily.

  • Dead or intermittent circuits
  • Breakers tripping or nuisance trips
  • Damaged receptacles, switches and devices
  • Lighting problems
  • Equipment and dedicated-circuit issues
  • Existing-system diagnostics
02

Panels, services & power

Build the path from source to load.

Power upgrades are more than swapping a panel. The service, feeders, overcurrent protection, grounding, available capacity, equipment requirements and future needs all affect the right solution.

Konnen evaluates the system around the upgrade so the finished work fits the load and the property instead of solving one limitation by creating another.

  • Panel replacements and upgrades
  • Service changes
  • Subpanels and feeder work
  • New dedicated circuits
  • Load-related upgrades
  • Distribution changes and equipment feeds
03

Remodels, additions & new circuits

Existing buildings require judgment.

Remodel electrical work has to connect new intent to existing conditions. Concealed wiring, limited pathways, old equipment, changed loads and other trades can all affect the plan.

Konnen works through those conditions deliberately—identifying what stays, what changes and how the new work integrates into the existing electrical system.

  • Kitchen and bathroom electrical
  • Additions and room changes
  • Shops and outbuildings
  • New receptacle and lighting circuits
  • Equipment circuits
  • Selective rewiring and electrical upgrades
04

Lighting, controls & equipment

The connection has to fit the equipment.

Lighting systems, motors, controls and dedicated equipment loads each come with their own requirements for power, protection, disconnecting means, control interfaces and installation conditions.

Konnen’s background includes equipment connections and control-oriented electrical work, bringing a broader systems view to these installations.

  • Interior and exterior lighting
  • Lighting controls and switching
  • Dedicated equipment circuits
  • Disconnects and local power
  • Motor and control-related electrical
  • Equipment replacement and upgrades
05

Commercial & project work

Electrical work that coordinates with the rest of the job.

Project work succeeds when the electrical scope is understood early enough to coordinate materials, routing, equipment, other trades and sequencing. That planning reduces rework and makes field decisions easier when conditions change.

Konnen can support selected project work where the scope, schedule and company capacity are a good fit.

  • Tenant improvements
  • Small-business electrical work
  • Remodel and build-out electrical
  • Plan-based electrical installation
  • Builder and trade coordination
  • Service and upgrade projects
