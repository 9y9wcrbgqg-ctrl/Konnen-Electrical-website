About Konnen

A new company.
Not new to the work.

Konnen Electrical was created to bring broad electrical experience, disciplined planning and straightforward service into one Southern Oregon company.

The meaning

Capability earned through knowledge, skill and experience.

Können is about ability—the kind that comes from knowing the work and being able to apply that knowledge when conditions are not perfect, the drawing does not tell the whole story or the problem is not obvious at first glance.

That idea fits electrical work. Good installation matters, but so do diagnosis, planning, judgment, sequencing and the ability to understand how one part of a system affects another.

Experience behind the company

More than one corner of the electrical trade.

The field experience behind Konnen spans residential construction and remodel work, commercial installation and service, industrial maintenance and troubleshooting, motors and control systems, low- and medium-voltage distribution, switchgear, grounding and bonding, and large mission-critical construction environments.

That variety matters because electrical work rarely exists in isolation. A service issue can involve loading. An equipment connection can involve controls. A remodel can expose old conditions. A project can succeed or fail based on coordination before the first conduit is installed.

Konnen brings that systems-level habit to the work without pretending the company itself has been around for decades. The company is new. The trade experience is established.
