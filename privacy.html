Website privacy

Privacy

A simple explanation of what happens when you use this website or prepare a project request.

Information you provide

If you use the project-request form, the information you enter is used to prepare an email from your own email application to Konnen Electrical. The website does not independently store the form contents in a database.

Direct communication

If you email or call Konnen Electrical, the information you provide may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, prepare an estimate, schedule work, maintain business records, or comply with legal obligations.

Website data

The hosting platform may process ordinary technical information needed to deliver and secure the website, such as IP address, browser information, request logs and performance data. Konnen does not use this website to sell personal information.

Third-party links

If the website links to a third-party service, that service’s own privacy terms apply after you leave this website.

Questions

For questions about information you have provided to Konnen Electrical, use the contact information shown on this website.
